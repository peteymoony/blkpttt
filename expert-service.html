What does the Expert Service cover?

Blockpit Experts set up the portfolio, review the transaction history, and clarify any open issues. The crypto tax report can then be generated and downloaded directly from the account.

How much do I still need to do myself?

Some input may be required when setting up integrations, providing missing data, or answering follow-up questions. The experts guide the necessary steps and handle the preparation.

How much does the Expert Service cost?

The initial consultation is free and provides a transparent assessment of the individual case. A tailored quote is then provided based on an hourly rate of €199.

Does the Expert Service replace tax advice?

No. The service covers the technical preparation of the data. For legally reliable tax advice, working with a specialized tax professional is still recommended. The completed report can then be provided to them.
