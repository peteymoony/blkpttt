What are Cookies?

Cookies are not delicious biscuits, but small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, smartphone or other end device, as well as in your browser. The next time you visit our website with the same terminal device, the information stored in Cookies is subsequently sent back either to our website ("First Party Cookie") or to another website to which the cookie belongs ("Third Party Cookie"). Cookies are used by Blockpit to provide a more user-friendly, effective and secure service. Cookies also allow Blockpit to identify the browser used and to make certain offers to the user. A further distinction is made between Session Cookies, which are temporary Cookies that are active during browsing and are deleted after closing your browser, and Persistent Cookies, which are stored in your browser until you delete them yourself or until your browser deletes them due to predefined settings. Most Cookies also have a unique identifier, a so-called "Cookie-ID". This allows websites to be assigned to the specific internet browser in which the Cookie was stored, whereby the identification of a certain person is usually only possible with certain third-party tools. You have the option, at any time, to reject the activation of specific Cookies by selecting the corresponding option in the browser settings or in the Cookie Settings.
